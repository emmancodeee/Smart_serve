## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Editing actions in the table
The Actions column SHALL contain exactly two compact equal-height buttons per row, horizontally aligned and centered: "Edit" (white/light background, yellow/gold accent, dark/yellow text) and "Archive" (white/light background, blue accent, blue text) for archiving feedback. No View/Details/Open or other actions SHALL be added, and no Delete action SHALL appear in the My Feedback section.

#### Scenario: Only Edit and Archive actions
- **WHEN** a feedback row's actions are inspected
- **THEN** only compact "Edit" and "Archive" buttons appear, equal in height, spaced apart, vertically centered
- **AND** no Delete button is present
